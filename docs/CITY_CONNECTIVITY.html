Why it looks like that

Streets start all over the map at once

  • generateEdges shuffles every node and grows one street from each, in that order. The first streets land scattered across the whole city, not spreading outward from one place.
  • A node gets a street either when its own turn comes or when someone else's street steps onto it. Until then it's a loose dot, which is what the screenshot catches.
  • So a live view of the street phase will always show loose dots mid-way. They're work that hasn't happened yet, not nodes that failed.

Nothing to fix here

I tested a second pass that grows streets again from every loose node and dead end. At seed 67 it adds 0 streets; at 36 starters it adds 6 of 18,961. The generator already connects every node it can.

If "not an adequate grid" meant the shape

The layout is organic rather than grid-like by design: random angles, 40–120 spacing, and a score of 1 × alignment + 4 × closeness (EDGE_ALIGNMENT_WEIGHT, EDGE_DISTANCE_WEIGHT). Closeness outweighs going straight, so streets turn toward the nearest node. If you want straighter, more grid-like streets, I can measure how those weights change the result. That would be a separate question.
